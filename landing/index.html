An agent that can't overspend.

Before the agent can buy anything, you set a limit: how much, apparel only, for 24 hours. It shops on its own virtual Visa card from Stripe, capped at that limit.

Ask it to "make it nicer" and the new cart goes over? It's refused, you're told why, and you get the closest outfit that fits. Only you can raise the limit.

Privacy

Your photos

Shrunk to small thumbnails on your phone. During a scan, the thumbnails are checked on our server and never stored. Full-size photos never leave your phone.

Your face

Used only to keep photos of you. Finding you computes face data for the people in your recent photos; none of it is stored, and yours is held in the app's memory for the session, then gone. Prefer not to? Pick your photos yourself instead.

Your spending limit

The agent never sees your card. Anything over your limit is refused, by us and by the card itself.

What we keep

Your style profile and closet tags, your outfit ratings and your spending limits, under your username. Never photos, never face data.

How we use AI

Our own trained model picks every outfit. Gemini reads photos into style tags and makes try-on images, which are AI previews, not photos. If our model is down, a backup fills in and the app says so.

Delete my data

One tap on the Trust screen deletes your profile, closet, ratings and account for good.
